# KAR Rebalanced
An admittedly small Riivolution patch designed for KAR-Deluxe (but still usable in vanilla) that rebalances most vehicles in subtle ways which emphasize the roles that each machine play. This also changes most of Kirby's walk params as well as some parameters pertaining to Dedede and MK.

Stars where I didn't notice flaws, major or minor, and thus didn't require any mitigations were hardly touched. These stars are:
- Jet star
- Turbo star
- Wagon star
- Compact Star
- Both legendary machines

I'm open to change, so feedback is appreciated.

## Installation
Clone into `KARphin\Mods\Load\Riivolution`, or whatever directory your Riivolution patches are loaded for your build.

For Netplay (or ease of access), save a Dolphin Game Mod Preset before starting, naming the json file "KarRebalance". Host or launch with this json.

## Known Issues
- Bulk star's drift isn't currently correct or "Riders Accurate".
- Bulk star struggles to move when it has too much Top Speed and Weight, with not enough Boost to compensate.
- Bulk star loses a slight amount of top speed if it has a lot of Boost. While this somewhat plays into its role as more of a fighter/tank than racer, its not ideal.
- Bulk star cannot accelerate at the ends of rail stations, due to the extreme ground friction there. May end up editing the city map.
